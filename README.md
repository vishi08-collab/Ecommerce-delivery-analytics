# E-Commerce Delivery Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce delivery performance using Microsoft Power BI. It explores delivery delays, warehouse performance, shipment modes, customer ratings, purchase behavior, product importance, and shipment weight to identify operational risks and opportunities for improvement.

The dashboard is organized into three report pages:
1. **E-Commerce Delivery Dashboard** — overall KPIs and delivery performance.
2. **Warehouse Performance Details** — shipment modes, customer ratings, purchase categories, and warehouse-related performance.
3. **Delivery Insights & Risk Analysis** — delayed orders, customer support calls, shipment weight, and delivery risks.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor on-time and delayed delivery performance.
- Identify shipment modes and order categories associated with delays.
- Understand the relationship between customer ratings and delivery outcomes.
- Analyze repeat-customer behavior and customer support calls.
- Identify high-risk shipments and recommend operational improvements.

## Key Insights
- **Delivery performance:** 59.67% of orders were delivered on time, while 40.33% were delayed.
- **Shipment mode:** Flight, ship, and road shipments show different delivery performance and should be monitored separately.
- **Shipment weight:** Very heavy shipments represent an important category for delay analysis and logistics planning.
- **Customer behavior:** Returning customers and customers making multiple support calls require closer analysis to understand repeat-delivery issues.
- **Purchase categories:** Delivery performance can be compared across returning, new, and loyal customer groups.
- **Customer experience:** Customer ratings provide an additional dimension for evaluating delivery outcomes.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate the causes of delays in road shipments and optimize routing and scheduling where needed.
2. Review handling capacity and logistics planning for very heavy shipments.
3. Monitor repeat-delivery issues affecting returning customers.
4. Investigate recurring support calls and improve proactive delivery notifications.
5. Track delivery KPIs regularly to identify performance changes and prioritize corrective actions.
